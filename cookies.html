Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This policy explains what this website stores in your browser and what it does not.

1. What we do not use

This site uses no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies. We do not run remarketing pixels, social pixels, or cross-site trackers.

2. What we do store

Small values are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device:

  • ebs_cookies: records your choice on our cookie banner.
  • Chat history and email: if you use the chat widget, your conversation and any email you share are stored locally so the chat can continue where you left off.
  • Theme preference: if you switch between dark and light theme.

None of these are sent to us unless you submit a form or share your email in the chat.

3. Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts: style files load from their CDN on every page. Font Awesome: the icon stylesheet loads from a CDN on the pages that use icons.
  • Calendly: when you open a booking page, Calendly may set its own cookies; Calendly's cookie policy applies on their pages.

4. Analytics

Analytics are currently disabled. No analytics cookies are set. If a real Google Analytics 4 ID is configured in the future, GA4 may set cookies, and we will update this policy and the consent banner before that happens.

5. Managing your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies for this site through your browser settings at any time. The banner stores your choice for future visits.
