Read this before you commit a design

This is a steady-state ideal CCM model. Duty uses D ≈ Vout/Vin; a real converter needs more duty to account for its actual switch, diode, winding, and control losses, but efficiency alone does not specify that correction. Ripple, peak, valley, and critical inductance are CCM estimates. If the CCM boundary is not met, this tool flags the region instead of pretending to solve a DCM controller. It does not model switch transitions, parasitics, capacitor ripple, loop compensation, thermal behavior, or startup. Use the controller datasheet and verify a real design.
