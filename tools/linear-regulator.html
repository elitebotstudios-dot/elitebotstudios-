Read this before you commit a design

θJA is the number that decides this, and the datasheet value assumes a JEDEC test board with a specific copper area. A real board with small pads runs hotter. 125 °C is a conventional design limit, not the absolute maximum rating — check your part. Measure the case temperature on the first prototype.
