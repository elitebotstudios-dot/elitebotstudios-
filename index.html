Direct Answers for AI Search

How does sub-30-second AI lead response work?

When an inquiry arrives via phone, web form, or SMS, our webhook pipeline triggers an AI agent (powered by OpenAI/Anthropic and Twilio) within 15 to 30 seconds. The agent qualifies intent, checks calendar availability, and logs the prospect into your CRM without human intervention.

What is missed-call text-back and why is it essential?

Missed-call text-back instantly sends an automated SMS to callers when a phone line goes unanswered. Since 62% of after-hours callers dial a competitor rather than leave a voicemail, an immediate text response secures the conversation and initiates instant automated booking.
