MSAEE · NUST
Founder

Muhammad Shahzaib Abbas

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan. My academic direction emphasizes power engineering, and I am independently developing complementary interests in embedded systems, electronics, microcontrollers, and control.

I am interested in how embedded computation can make electrical and energy systems more observable and controllable. Elite Bot Studios gives me a place to document that development through practical designs, simulations, prototypes, and engineering notes—clearly separating what is planned from what has been built and measured.

My aim is to build useful technical depth over time and contribute to thoughtful engineering and research collaborations. I am also interested in further study and international research, particularly in Europe including Germany, as my academic path develops. This site will grow as verified projects, academic work, and supporting evidence become available.
