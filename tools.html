Tools

Calculators we actually use.

Six tools for the questions that come up on every board: how wide the copper has to be, how hot the regulator will run, how much ripple the inductor will pass. They run entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Available calculators

Why these are free

They are the same calculations used when scoping a build, and putting them in the open is the cheapest way to show how the work is done. No account, no email gate, no tracking.

What they are not

Every one of these is a closed-form estimate. None of them replaces a field solver, a thermal test on a real board, or the datasheet of the part you actually bought. Each page says exactly where its model breaks down — read that note before you commit a design.
