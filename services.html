Collaboration

What a useful conversation looks like.

The studio is run by one undergraduate engineer, so the honest offer is attention rather than scale: technical discussion, careful review, and clearly scoped low-voltage work.

WHO GETS IN TOUCH

Four kinds of
conversation.

If your question is technical, say what you are measuring, building, or trying to understand. That is usually enough to start.

01

Research and academic discussion

Questions about embedded control, energy instrumentation or power conversion where a student perspective, a literature pointer, or a second pair of eyes is useful.

  • Discussing a paper or approach
  • Reviewing a project plan or a test method
  • Pointing at relevant work worth reading
02

Student and early-career collaboration

Working alongside another student on a shared technical problem, usually with clearly split responsibilities and a written scope.

  • Shared simulation or documentation work
  • Peer review of schematics or firmware structure
  • Study groups around a specific topic
03

Internships and graduate opportunities

Placements where a supervised team can help an undergraduate develop hardware and firmware judgement.

  • Embedded or power-electronics internship
  • Research assistant or lab support role
  • Graduate scheme with a hardware track
04

Low-voltage hardware conversations

Early-stage questions about a small embedded or low-voltage power design, where a careful second opinion is more useful than a quotation.

  • Sensing and measurement approach
  • Board-level review before fabrication
  • Firmware structure and bring-up planning
SCOPE

What is in, and what is not.

  • In scope: low-voltage embedded and power-electronics work, simulation studies, documentation, and review — the same territory as the tools and notes on this site.
  • Needs supervision: anything mains-connected, high-voltage, safety-critical, or requiring regulatory approval. That is not offered for unsupervised commissioning.
  • Not claimed: professional licensure, a team, an established consultancy, or delivered client projects.
