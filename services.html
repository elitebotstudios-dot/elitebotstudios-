Services

Four things we do, and do properly.

Every engagement ends with working hardware and the documentation to maintain it. Scope, parts list, and price are agreed before work starts.

01

Embedded Firmware Development

C and C++ firmware for microcontrollers — written to run for years, not to pass a demo. Drivers, state machines, and real-time task structure, with the failure modes handled.

  • Firmware architecture and task design
  • Peripheral drivers (I²C, SPI, UART, ADC, PWM)
  • FreeRTOS integration and timing analysis
  • Over-the-air update paths
  • Serial logging and on-device diagnostics
  • Source, build instructions, and a flashed reference board
ESP32 · STM32 · C/C++ · FreeRTOS · PlatformIO · ESP-IDF · CMSIS
02

PCB Design (KiCad)

Schematic capture through to manufacturing output. Layout is done with current return paths, thermal spreading, and the enclosure in mind — not just connectivity.

  • Schematic capture and component selection
  • Two and four layer layout
  • Ground plane strategy and decoupling
  • Design-rule and manufacturability checks
  • Gerbers, drill files, and fabrication notes
  • Bill of materials with supplier part numbers
KiCad · DRC/ERC · Gerber & Excellon · FR-4 · ENIG
03

IoT Device Prototyping

Connected devices that report reliably and recover on their own. Provisioning, transport, and the boring parts that decide whether a deployment survives contact with a real network.

  • Wi-Fi and BLE connectivity
  • MQTT publish/subscribe architecture
  • Device provisioning and credentials
  • Reconnect and watchdog behaviour
  • Local buffering for dropped links
  • Dashboard-ready telemetry format
ESP32 · MQTT · TLS · Wi-Fi/BLE · REST · JSON
04

Power Electronics Prototyping

Regulation, conversion, and protection. Bench-measured efficiency and thermal behaviour, not just a simulated result.

  • Buck, boost, and linear regulation
  • Battery charging and protection
  • Load switching and relay drive
  • Efficiency and thermal measurement
  • Ripple, transient, and stability checks
  • Test report with scope captures
Buck/Boost · LDO · MOSFET drive · BMS · Thermal design
How remote delivery works

Three stages. No surprises.

You get a fixed scope, a parts list (BOM) with supplier links, and a wiring diagram. No surprises.
