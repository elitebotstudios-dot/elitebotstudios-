01 / BACKGROUND

Power systems drew me in.
Embedded control keeps widening the frame.

I study Electrical Engineering at the National University of Sciences and Technology in Pakistan, with an academic direction in power engineering. Alongside my degree, I am building a complementary understanding of embedded systems, electronics, microcontrollers, and control.

I am drawn to the point where an electrical system becomes observable and responsive: sensing what is happening, deciding what should happen next, and understanding the limits of the hardware doing it. Elite Bot Studios is my long-term place to develop those ideas in public through models, project records, and technical notes.

My broader interests include power electronics, intelligent energy systems, and applied research. I am interested in postgraduate study and international research collaboration, particularly in Europe and Germany, as my academic path develops.
