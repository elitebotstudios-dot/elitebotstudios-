Cookies

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

This site sets a small number of cookies, all for measurement. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookies we set

  • _ga — Google Analytics 4. Distinguishes one browser from another. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_HLQ4G5HBMZ — Google Analytics 4. Keeps session state for this property. Expires after 2 years.

What we do not set

No advertising cookies, no social media pixels, no cross-site tracking, and no profiling cookies.

How to refuse them

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect how the site works. You can also install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
