Read this before you commit a design

This is an ideal two-level waveform with zero rise and fall time. Real GPIO outputs have finite edges, driver delay, and loading; a gate driver, LED string, motor or RC filter each responds to the waveform differently. The average is what a slow load integrates, not what a fast one sees. Nothing here is a measurement.
