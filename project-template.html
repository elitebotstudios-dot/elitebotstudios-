How a project record is built.

Every engineering record on this site follows the same structure, so a reader can compare a concept, a simulation, and a measured system on equal terms. Sections only appear when they contain real content.

01 / STRUCTURE

Objective, method, evidence, limits.

A record answers four questions in order: what problem is being addressed, how it was approached, what evidence exists, and where the work stops. Status labels keep those stages honest.

Download the Markdown template ↗

The download is the blank authoring template kept in the repository. It is a starting structure, not a completed project.

  1. 01

    Overview and objective

    The system, the engineering question, and the conditions the design must respect.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Signal and power flow, drawn as a diagram only when a real design or an explicitly labelled concept exists.

  3. 03

    Hardware and software

    Confirmed parts, revisions, firmware structure, interfaces, and toolchain.

  4. 04

    Design decisions

    Trade-offs considered, calculations used, and alternatives rejected.

  5. 05

    Simulation

    Model, inputs, assumptions, and outputs, always identified as simulated.

  6. 06

    Testing and validation

    Fixture, instruments, operating conditions, method, and acceptance criteria.

  7. 07

    Results and limitations

    Measured values kept separate from expected and simulated ones, with the limits stated plainly.

  8. 08

    Resources

    Repositories, documents, images, and demonstrations—only when they exist and can be shared.
