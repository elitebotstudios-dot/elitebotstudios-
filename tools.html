Tools

Small tools for early estimates.

Six browser-based calculators for common early estimates: copper width, regulator dissipation, converter ripple, resistor selection, and first-order RC behaviour. Inputs are processed in your browser; they are not sent to a calculation API.

Why these are free

These lightweight educational calculators make common engineering equations easier to inspect and test. Inputs stay in the browser and are not sent to a calculation API. No account or email gate is required.

What they are not

Every one of these is a closed-form estimate. None of them replaces a field solver, a thermal test on a real board, or the datasheet of the part you actually bought. Each page says exactly where its model breaks down — read that note before you commit a design.
