Capabilities & collaboration

Areas to explore together.

Elite Bot Studios is an independent, evolving engineering initiative—not an established consultancy. These topics describe current academic focus and collaboration interests; they are not a promise of qualifications, certification, or unsupervised delivery.

01 / FOCUS

Embedded systems

Microcontroller architecture, firmware structure, peripheral communication, and timing are active study areas. Specific demonstrated work is linked only when a project record supports it.

  • Microcontroller fundamentals
  • Firmware structure and state machines
  • UART, SPI, I²C, ADC, PWM concepts
02 / FOCUS

Power electronics

Power conversion and control are the academic centre of gravity: understanding switching behaviour, regulation, and the trade-offs behind converter design.

  • DC–DC conversion principles
  • Switching devices and gate-drive concepts
  • Model-based analysis and validation questions
03 / FOCUS

Electronics & PCB

Circuit design and board-level implementation connect system requirements to buildable hardware. Design files and fabrication evidence will be published when verified.

  • Circuit analysis and component selection
  • Schematic and PCB workflow
  • Protection, grounding, and measurement
04 / FOCUS

Control, instrumentation & IoT

Control and sensing link electrical behaviour with embedded computation. Connected-energy systems remain a direction of exploration, not a claim of deployed products.

  • Feedback and control fundamentals
  • Sensors and signal conditioning
  • Connected-device and energy-monitoring concepts
Scope responsibly

Any practical engagement is discussed case by case against demonstrated experience. High-voltage, mains-connected, regulated, and safety-critical work is not offered for unsupervised commissioning. The portfolio distinguishes academic study, concepts, simulation, and measured hardware.
