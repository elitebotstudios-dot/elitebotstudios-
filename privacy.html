Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Elite Bot Studios ("we") operates elitebotstudios.me. This page explains what is collected when you use the site and what happens to it.

What is collected

  • Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HLQ4G5HBMZ) to record aggregate usage: pages viewed, approximate region, device type, and referring site.
  • Contact enquiries. If you email us or submit the contact form, we receive the details you provide — your name, email address, and project description.
  • Server logs. Our host (Vercel) records standard request logs, including IP address, for security and abuse prevention.

What is not collected

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not run advertising pixels, behavioural profiling, or cross-site tracking. There are no third-party marketing cookies on this site.

Why it is collected

Analytics tell us which pages are useful. Enquiry details are used only to answer the enquiry and, if you engage us, to deliver the work. Server logs are used to keep the site available and secure.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies. See the Cookies page for the specific names and how to refuse them.

Retention

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of work agreed. Analytics data is retained according to Google's default retention period.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. Email contact@elitebotstudios.me and we will respond within 30 days.

Third parties

  • Google Analytics — usage measurement
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Google Fonts — typeface delivery

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above will change with it.

Contact
